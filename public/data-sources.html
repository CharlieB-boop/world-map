Map and country data

This is an independent first-stage PSS International Removals prototype. The service list and branding require company review.

  • Map geometry: Natural Earth 1:50m public-domain country boundaries, distributed by world-atlas. ISC license. Borders reflect the source dataset and are not a statement about disputed territories.
  • Country names, capitals, ISO identifiers, flags and approximate centres: mledoze/countries via world-countries. Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. The derived country database, including local additions, is available as destinations.json under the same ODbL license.
  • Populations: manually rounded, 2024-era prototype context. These are illustrative demographic approximations rather than live or authoritative statistics, and should be checked before publication.
  • Moving price and transit guides were checked against published PSS International Removals pages on 20 October 2026. Each entry includes its source and scope in moving-guides.json. These are planning guides, not live quotations or statistical averages. Some tables carry older dates, shown in the interface. Exact two-bedroom prices are unavailable for most destinations; India and Singapore references cover two to three bedrooms. Box examples retain their published quantities. Missing researched prices and times are filled with illustrative prototype budgets: invented regional defaults and country overrides, explicitly labelled in the interface. These are not web research, PSS prices, averages or delivery commitments. The service list remains provisional; verified page links do not confirm current availability.
